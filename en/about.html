About

Spadchyna is a way of looking.

Spadchyna is a static guide to places of memory and cultural heritage in Belarus. It connects two layers: the official State List of Historical and Cultural Values and twenty-one curated stories in which people, places and landscapes become visible behind dates and reference codes.

This is an educational non-commercial project. It is not an official website of state bodies.

Idea

Memory is often shown as a list of dates. We propose another view: a place, its surroundings, a photograph, an official document and a route. The map helps to see that the history of war sits alongside the history of castles, churches and towns — and that heritage is not divided into “military” and “peaceful”.

Hence four tools: a map with filters, a catalogue with official records, ready-made walks by time and a personal route you can build for your own trip.

Data

The main layer is objects from the State List of Historical and Cultural Values of the Republic of Belarus. Names, addresses, categories, reference codes and images come from the open catalogue heritage.gov.by. Object descriptions are given in the original language — Belarusian.

The stories rely on official museum, memorial and government websites. Source links are included in every record and on the Sources and credits page. We do not invent facts or replace official texts with a retelling where accuracy matters.

The site is not an official publication of state bodies. Before using the materials in educational work, check the primary sources.

Limitations

  • The main map shows regional borders and object points but does not replace a navigator. Walk maps use OpenStreetMap and are a guide, not a navigation tool.
  • Route distance is measured in a straight line, not by roads; use the “Route in Yandex Maps” link for streets.
  • Some objects in the official catalogue do not yet have a downloaded text description; verified fields and a link to the original source are provided instead.
  • Wikimedia Commons images are used under CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 / public domain licences; museum photos are credited to their source.
