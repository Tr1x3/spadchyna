Yama

Memorial to the victims of the Minsk ghetto

A memorial on the site of the mass shooting of Minsk ghetto prisoners on 2 March 1942. One of the first Holocaust memorials in the USSR.

The Yama memorial in Minsk is located on Melnikaite Street, on the site of the former Jewish ghetto. On 2 March 1942 about five thousand prisoners were killed here.

The first obelisk appeared on this site in 1947. It was one of the first Holocaust memorials in the USSR and the first to be allowed an inscription in Yiddish.

In 2000 the memorial gained the sculptural composition “The Last Road” by Leonid Levin — a group of doomed people descending to the bottom of the pit.

The memorial remains a place of memory for the tragedy of the Minsk ghetto and one of the stops on a route through the Jewish history of Belarus.
