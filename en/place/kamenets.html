Kamenets Tower

A 13th-century Volhynian tower

The only surviving Volhynian tower in the western lands, built in the 13th–14th centuries. Its height is 29.4 metres.

Kamenets Tower is a monument of defensive architecture from the second half of the 13th century. It was built on a high hill on the left bank of the Lesnaya River.

It is the only surviving Volhynian tower erected in the western lands in the 13th–14th centuries. The tower stood inside a ring rampart and dominated the wooden fortifications.

The five-tier tower is 29.4 metres high, its walls are 2.5 metres thick, and its outer diameter is 13.6 metres. It stands on a massive stone foundation.

Today it is a museum and one of the main sights of Kamenets. The tower offers a view of the town and its surroundings.
