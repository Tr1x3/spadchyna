Vasilishki

Mass grave of ghetto prisoners

In the centre of Vasilishki is a mass grave of ghetto prisoners shot on 10 May 1943. An obelisk was installed in 1967.

Vasilishki is an agro-town in the Shchuchyn district of the Grodno region. Before the war a large Jewish community lived here, and the town was a district centre.

After the occupation in 1941 the Nazis established a ghetto. Prisoners were held in overcrowded conditions, driven to forced labour every day and forbidden to leave the ghetto under threat of execution.

On 10 May 1943 the ghetto was liquidated. People were driven from their homes, beaten and herded to the cemetery, where they were shot. Among those who died were relatives of one of the few survivors, Samuil Polyachek.

In 1967 an obelisk was installed at the execution site. Today it is a place of memory for the Jewish community of Vasilishki and the victims of the Holocaust.
