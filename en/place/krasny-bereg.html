Krasny Bereg

“To Children — Victims of War” memorial

A memorial in the Zhlobin district dedicated to children affected by Nazi policy. The “To Children of War” museum also operates here.

Krasny Bereg in the Zhlobin district is a place of memory for children affected by Nazi policy. During the war a transit camp operated here, through which children were taken for forced labour in Germany.

The “To Children — Victims of War” memorial complex opened on 28 June 2007. Its author is architect Leonid Mendelevich Levin, Honoured Architect of the Republic of Belarus.

The composition is conceived as a “City of the Sun”: rays and alleys radiate from the central “Square of the Sun”, while a black “Ray of Memory” leads to the entrance. At the centre is a figure of a girl symbolising children deprived of childhood.

The “To Children of War” museum operates at the memorial. Its exhibition tells about pre-war childhood, the occupation, the partisan movement, the camp at Krasny Bereg and children forced into labour in Germany.
