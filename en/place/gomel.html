Gomel Palace

The Rumyantsev and Paskevich palace and park ensemble

One of the most interesting monuments of estate architecture in Belarus, combining classicism and eclecticism.

Gomel Palace is one of the most interesting monuments of estate architecture in Belarus from the late 18th to the mid-19th century. It is notable for its richness and plasticity, combining classicism and eclecticism.

The palace is associated with the Rumyantsev and Paskevich families. Architects and decorators from Russia, Poland, England and Italy, as well as local craftsmen, were invited to take part in its construction.

The palace architecture is closely linked to the surrounding landscape. The complex includes a park, the Saints Peter and Paul Cathedral, the Paskevich chapel-tomb and outbuildings.

Today it is one of Gomel’s main museums and a landmark on the Sozh embankment. The palace and park remain the city’s calling card.
