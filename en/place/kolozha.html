Kolozha Church

A 12th-century church on the Neman

The Boris and Gleb (Kolozha) Church is one of the oldest surviving churches in Belarus and a monument of the Grodno architectural school.

The Boris and Gleb (Kolozha) Church stands on the right bank of the Neman in Grodno, near the mouth of the Haradnichanka. It was built in the 1180s and belongs to the Grodno architectural school.

The builders used plinth bricks, voice vessels, dressed stone and lime mortar. The facades and floor were decorated with majolica tiles; the walls were painted with frescoes, of which a small fragment survives.

The church is one of the oldest surviving churches in Belarus and is an object of the State List of Historical and Cultural Values.

Today it is an active church and one of the main symbols of Grodno. The Old Castle and the Neman embankment are nearby.
