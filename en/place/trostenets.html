Trostenets

The largest death camp in the USSR

A death camp near Minsk where about 206,500 people were killed between 1942 and 1944. The memorial complex opened in 2015.

Maly Trostenets near Minsk was the largest death camp in the occupied territory of the USSR. It operated from spring 1942 to summer 1944.

Civilians, Soviet prisoners of war, Jews from Belarus, Austria, Germany and Czechia, partisans and underground fighters were murdered here. According to the 1944 Extraordinary State Commission, about 206,500 people died.

In 2015 a memorial complex opened on the historical site. Its central composition is the ten-metre “Gates of Memory” by sculptor Konstantin Kostyuchenko; the “Array of Names” monument stands nearby.

The memorial is located on the south-eastern outskirts of Minsk. It is a place of European memory visited by descendants of the victims from different countries.
